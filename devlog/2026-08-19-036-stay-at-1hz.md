# 036 · Telemetry stays at 1 Hz

**Date** 2026-08-19
**Type** decision
**Refs** ISS-13

## What

2 Hz telemetry was considered and **rejected**. `CYCLE_PERIOD_MS` stays at 1000. No
firmware change, no reflash.

Recorded so it is not proposed again without new information — specifically, without a
way to buy back the cycle time.

## Why

The motivation was real: higher data resolution, and at 1 Hz rotation above 180 deg/s is
beyond Nyquist, which is why integrated yaw is flagged unresolvable during fast tumbling.

The obstacle is the cycle budget, documented in `Config.h:31-33` and measured on hardware:

```
listen 400 + sensors 15 + transmit 231 = 646 ms, leaving 354 ms
```

**646 ms is already spent of a 1000 ms cycle.** At 2 Hz the cycle is 500 ms, so it
overruns by 146 ms every single time. Setting `CYCLE_PERIOD_MS` to 500 alone would put the
unit into permanent overrun — the condition entry 021 added reporting for.

Three ways to close the 146 ms, and every one of them costs something:

| Option | Result | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Listen window 400 → 150 ms | Fits, ~104 ms slack | Narrower window for EJECT and PING to land |
| BW250 instead of 125 | 531 ms — still overruns | ~3 dB sensitivity, and does not even solve it alone |
| Both | 281 ms, ~219 ms slack | Both costs |
| Trim packet fields | ~595 ms — not enough | Confirmed: even dropping the dead GPS fields leaves a gap |

The listen window is the expensive one to touch because it does **two** jobs. From
`MRC_FlightUnit_GEN3.ino:111`: GPS is fed on every tick inside it, and at 9600 baud the
UART FIFO fills in about 130 ms. So it is the uplink window *and* the GPS servicing window,
and it cannot go much below 150 ms without dropping NMEA sentences once the GPS works.

**Aiman's decision: not worth narrowing the parachute command's window, or losing range,
for double the sample rate.**

## Result

Nothing changed. The value of writing it down is the reasoning, not the outcome.

Two consequences to expect rather than treat as faults:

- **Rotation above 180 deg/s stays unresolvable.** `ALIAS_RATE` in `attitude.ts` will flag
  yaw as degraded during fast tumbling, which is correct — the samples genuinely alias.
- **Every 1 Hz assumption in the dashboard is now correct rather than fragile.**
  `RATE_WINDOW = 5` really is a 5 s window; `DEFAULT_WINDOW = 60` really is a minute;
  `LINK_STALE_MS = 3000` really is three missed packets. The plan to make these
  rate-derived was proposed for a rate change that is not happening, and is dropped.

**The frequency change is unaffected and still open.** It is a separate, free, one-line
edit in each `Config.h` and it addresses what is actually costing packets: roughly 75% lost
to collisions on a shared 919.0 MHz (`ISS-13`). Clearing the channel is worth about four
times the data that doubling the rate would have been — at 1 Hz with 75% loss the dashboard
receives ~0.25 samples/s; clean, it receives ~1.0; at 2 Hz on a congested channel, ~0.5.
Waiting on a frequency to move to.
