## What

Rewrote `backend/dashboard/parser.py` to accept GEN3 alongside GEN1 and GEN2, with CRC16
verification. Step 1 of `wiki/decisions/dashboard-gen3-plan.md`.

Also tagged the two deferred pieces as issues so they stop living in conversation:
`ISS-15` (SQLite store) and `ISS-16` (replay).

## Result

**Verified against real hardware data, not fixtures.**

| Input | Result |
|---|---|
| `logs/raw/FLIGHT22.CSV` — 85 real SD packets | **85/85 parsed**, `crc_ok=True` |
| Over-the-air packet with RSSI/SNR appended | parsed, `seq=661`, `ms=668683` |
| Single flipped bit | **rejected**, `computed D42A, packet says 3E1A` |
| GEN2 and GEN1 lines | still parse, unchanged |
| SD `#` header lines | classified as status, not errors |

All 22 pre-existing tests still pass.

Decisions from the plan that became code:

- **A failed checksum reports no sequence data** (S1). `seq` and `vehicle_ms` come back
  `None` on a CRC failure rather than the values that happened to be in the corrupt
  packet. Trusting a sequence number from a failed checksum is exactly how a phantom gap
  or a false vehicle-restart gets into the link statistics — and it would do so precisely
  when the link is worst.
- **A failed checksum rejects the frame** (S6): `ok=False`, `frame=None`, raw line still
  carried so the feed can show it. Same treatment as a malformed line, same reason — a
  corrupt packet must never move the altitude trace.
- **`seq` and `vehicle_ms` are envelope-level, not frame fields.** The `frame` dict stays
  a pure measurement payload matching the frontend's `TelemetryFrame`; identity and timing
  belong beside it.

**GEN3's link-quality fields are optional**, which was not in the original plan and turned
out to matter. The ground station appends RSSI and SNR; a packet read straight off the SD
card has neither. Since that is the same file a replay source would consume (`ISS-16`),
supporting both shapes now means replay needs no special case later — and it is why
`FLIGHT22.CSV` could be used as the test corpus at all.

When absent they are `None`, never `0`. A zero would render as a genuine −0 dBm reading,
which is a fabricated measurement.
